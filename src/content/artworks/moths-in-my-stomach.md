Senior year BAS; Medium sized abstract painting.

“[Moths] In My Stomach”

My face turning red made my stomach go blue
as blood rushed to my head and filled up the room.

But I felt them first and the journey they made
from their cocoons they burst and fell from my brain.

Hearts starts to race preparing me to fight
While they make their escape to take their first flight.

But i can feel each one crawling past my throat  
With no light or sun it seems they’ve entered a hoax.

The stomach is dark with infinite earthquakes
But its blood captured in part has left it unawake.

Into the cold room they fall not yet so bold 
Climbing the rugae walls of mucus lined folds.
                            
Now adrenaline takes control and disrupts the herd
Freezing time whole by holding the vagus nerve.

Panic ensues and mirrors my complexion
Each other they pursued and flew with misdirection.

Fear froze the belly but dazzled them awake
With bodies so hairy and the flutters they make.

Despite how little i caught i found it scenic 
Because I felt a moth or three in my stomach.
